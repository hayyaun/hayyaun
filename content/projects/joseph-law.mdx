## Overview

Joseph Law is a law firm website. This case study examines how content hierarchy, service information, and contact paths support visitors looking for legal help.

## Role and challenge

My role was frontend implementation. The central challenge was balancing a distinctive visual presentation with clear reading order and straightforward access to contact information. The interface needed to remain understandable across mobile and desktop layouts.

## Implementation

I treated navigation, text hierarchy, and contact actions as core interface elements. Reusable layout rules kept information consistent across the site, while responsive spacing and typography preserved readability as the available width changed.
